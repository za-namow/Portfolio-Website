About me

Tachawut Hirunburana

Hello there! My name is Tachawut Hirunburana, though I go by Namo. I graduated from Saint Gabriel’s College and enrolled in Science – Mathematics program, known in school as Engineering and Applied Science or EAS for short. There are many things to love in life, but there's one thing that I love the most: teamwork. I love how we have to work in pairs or teams to accomplish something better, with many ideas being thrown around and then developed into a project/work. If we’re talking about the topic that I enjoy working in the most in computer engineering, that would be either IoT or Robotics. I love the feeling of working on-site and getting my hands dirty, but going slow and steady like website-development is also a break from the stress too, in my humble opinion.
